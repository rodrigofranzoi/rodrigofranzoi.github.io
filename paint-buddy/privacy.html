Política de privacidade

Última atualização: 7 de setembro de 2026

本ポリシーは、Rodrigo Franzoi Scroferneker（「当社」）が開発した Paint Buddy（「本アプリ」）が Mac 上で情報をどのように取り扱うかを説明します。本アプリを利用することで、これらの取扱いに同意したものとみなします。

1. 管理者

The controller of any personal data processed is Rodrigo Franzoi Scroferneker. Contact: rodrigo.scroferneker@gmail.com.

2. 概要

Paint Buddy monitors the clipboard only to detect color strings and keeps a local history on your Mac. It can also save colors from the system picker. Analytics and Crashlytics (Firebase) send technical events — never full clipboard contents. Screen Recording is not required and colors are not synced to the cloud.

3. 本アプリが行わないこと

  • It does not create our own accounts or require sign-in to us.
  • It does not ask for your name, tax ID or home address.
  • It does not sell data and does not show third-party ads.
  • It does not send sensitive app content (full clipboard text beyond locally detected color tokens) to our servers or to Firebase.
  • It does not use Screen Recording.

4. Mac に保存されるデータ

  • Color history: color values and metadata (hex/rgb/rgba/name) in UserDefaults — on-device, without a dedicated encrypted database.
  • Preferences: capture formats, copy format, history limits, pause, launch at login, appearance and floating palette options.

You can delete this data by clearing history, uninstalling the App or, when available, in settings.

5. サービス経由で送信される情報

5.1. Firebase Analytics. Google Firebase may receive anonymous/technical usage events (for example, app launch or color saved with source “clipboard”/“picker”, without the color value). Full clipboard text is never sent.

5.2. Firebase Crashlytics. Crash reports and technical breadcrumbs may be sent. They do not include color history.

5.3. Apple. Distribution via the Mac App Store follows Apple’s rules.

6. 目的と法的根拠

We use information to:

  • provide color history on your Mac;
  • improve the product with analytics;
  • diagnose failures with Crashlytics.

In Brazil, processing follows Law No. 13.709/2018 (LGPD). We do not sell your data and do not use it for third-party ads.

7. 共有

We share information only as needed to operate the App:

  • Apple: delivering the app on the Mac App Store;
  • Google (Firebase): analytics and Crashlytics;
  • when required by law or to defend legal rights.

8. 保持

Data on your Mac remains until you delete it or uninstall the App. Crash reports and analytics events follow Firebase/Google retention. Uninstalling removes local data but does not automatically erase what was already sent to those services.

9. お客様の権利

You can clear local history or uninstall. For access, correction or deletion requests, email rodrigo.scroferneker@gmail.com. We will respond as reasonably required under applicable law (including LGPD).

10. 子ども

The App does not profile children and is not directed at anyone under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe data was sent, contact us.

11. セキュリティと移転

Color history stays on your Mac. No internet transfer is risk-free. Firebase and the App Store may process technical information on servers outside Brazil; see those providers’ policies for safeguards.

12. 変更

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the current version. Continued use after publication means you are aware of the new text.

13. Contato

Rodrigo Franzoi Scroferneker
rodrigo.scroferneker@gmail.com
